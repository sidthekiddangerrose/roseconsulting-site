Featured project

Emberfall

Every strike is a test of timing. Every dying flame brings the darkness closer.

Explore a dying world in this illustrated, story-driven fantasy RPG. Discover hidden relics, forge powerful rune combinations, and master precision-timed combat. As Alden, uncover your mother's legacy and carry the last flame toward a final confrontation with Varos.

Coming soon on Steam

View Emberfall on Steam

  • Master the art of combat

    Attack, Guard and Focus each use an interactive timing bar. Land Normal, Strong or Perfect actions, or miss your opportunity entirely.

  • Forge your own fighting style

    Equip one of four primary rune shards, pair it with one of three secondary runes, and experiment with 12 rune combinations.

  • Keep the last flame alive

    Travel, rest, retreat and defeat all take their toll on your Ember Flame. Keep it burning to strengthen your attacks.

  • Explore a world buried in ash

    Travel through 25 interconnected locations in illustrated, text-driven scenes, from a ruined hunter's camp to the storm-shrouded ridge.

Journal & bestiary

Follow your journal as the world reveals its secrets, and record what you learn about each enemy in a growing bestiary.
